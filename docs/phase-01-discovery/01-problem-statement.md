# Problem Statement

**Client:** A local fitness studio operating multiple locations.

**Current state:** Class bookings, trainer scheduling, and member communication are handled manually — a mix of spreadsheets, calendars, and phone calls.

**Consequences of the current state:**

1. **Inefficient operations** — manual booking causes delays, double-booked classes, and missed appointments.
2. **Poor member experience** — no online booking; inconsistent communication about class details/updates; no loyalty program to encourage repeat visits.
3. **Lack of business insight** — management has no real-time visibility into class popularity, attendance, or trainer performance.
4. **No engagement strategy** — nothing structured to retain or motivate members.

**Goal:** Implement a Salesforce-based platform that becomes the single source of truth for member and class management — centralizing operations, automating key processes, and surfacing business insight through dashboards and reports.

**Success looks like:**

- Members can browse, book, and cancel classes online, and see their loyalty points.
- Staff have one place to manage classes, trainers, and attendance.
- Management can see attendance, class popularity, and trainer performance at a glance.
- Loyalty points accrue automatically from attendance — no manual tracking.
